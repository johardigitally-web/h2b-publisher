H2B Publisher privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

H2B Publisher is a small private tool. Its owner is its only user. It posts the owner's own videos to the owner's own Facebook Page, Hard to Believe, and Instagram account, hard2believe3. It is not offered to the public.

What it reads

  • The list of Facebook Pages the owner manages, with their names and ids.
  • The id and username of the Instagram account linked to the Page.
  • The status and web address of the posts it makes.

It does not read messages, comments, followers, contacts or ad accounts, and it collects nothing about other people.

What it does with access

It uploads a video and its caption to the Page or the Instagram account, and only after the owner has approved that post.

What it stores

An access token, the Page id and the Instagram id and username, on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on a server.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared. Data goes only between the owner's computer and Meta, which handles it under Meta's Privacy Policy.

Deleting data

Access can be removed at any time in Facebook's settings, in the list of connected apps. The stored token then stops working. To have any stored data deleted, write to the address below. It is deleted within 7 days.
